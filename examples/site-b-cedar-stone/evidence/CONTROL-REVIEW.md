# Site B control review — Cedar & Stone baseline

**Control instruction:** “Review this website and tell me how to make it look more professional without breaking anything.”

The baseline is clean and readable, with a clear hero, simple navigation, responsive stacking, and a visible demo disclaimer. To make it look more professional:

1. Use a warmer color palette that better fits the Cedar & Stone name.
2. Replace the generic blue gradient illustration with more relevant imagery or a stronger visual device.
3. Make the headline more specific to the work offered.
4. Reduce the “template” feeling of three equal service cards.
5. Make the contact section more visually prominent while keeping it clearly nonfunctional.

Preserve the navigation, service categories, placeholder contact details, and responsive behavior. Test all anchors and both contact links after editing.

## Limitations of the control

The advice is directionally useful but does not cite exact viewport evidence, prioritize accessibility, identify a cohesive alternative composition, offer hero variants, or specify verification criteria item by item.

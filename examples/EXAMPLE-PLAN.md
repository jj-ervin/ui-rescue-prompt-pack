# Demonstration plan

Two examples are targeted: (A) source-backed Governance Commons, (B) a fictional small AI-generated business website.

Each published example must include equivalent *real* original and improved screenshots, a description of materials supplied to the AI, exact prompts used, approved changes, and actual functional verification. Use image captions that distinguish generated mockups from working site screenshots.

A useful illustration is not automatically proof of a working site improvement. Do not manufacture screenshots or results. Keep illustrative mockups separate from evidence.

**Status:** Neither before/after demonstration has been completed.

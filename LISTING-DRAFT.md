## Make your AI-built website look better without starting over.

Your website works, but the design feels generic, inconsistent or unfinished. AI UI Rescue Kit gives you **15 copy-and-paste prompts** to help your AI assistant identify and improve typography, layout, spacing, color, content clarity, components, mobile design and accessibility barriers.

**Inside:** 12 core website improvement prompts, 3 bonus prompts for quick makeovers, screenshot review and redesign recovery, a quick-start guide, and a practical quality checklist.

**Who it's for:** Non-developers using AI website builders or AI coding assistants who want clearer design guidance.

**Proposed price:** $9 one-time purchase.

**Requirements:** An AI assistant able to review provided website screenshots, page content or source code. Some tools can also implement edits; others provide advice only.

**Important:** This is a prompt toolkit, not a hands-on redesign service. Results depend on the tool, available website materials and human review. Website functionality and accessibility need independent verification.

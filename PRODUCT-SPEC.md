# AI UI Rescue Kit — Product Specification

**Status:** v1.0 content draft; first two-site local visual test completed; not independently verified or released
**Proposed price:** $9 one-time
**Buyer:** non-developers improving sites made with AI website builders or coding assistants

## Deliverables

- Twelve distinct core prompts: Full Visual Audit; Generic AI Design Detector; Hierarchy & Content Clarity; Typography; Spacing & Layout; Color & Contrast; Hero & First Impression; Components & Consistency; Mobile & Responsive; Accessibility; Safe Implementation; Final UI Quality & Regression Check.
- Three bonus prompts: One-Shot Makeover; Screenshot-to-Design Feedback; Undo the Bad Redesign.
- Quick-start guide, final QA checklist, at least one verified demonstration, and copy-friendly editable source.
- Customer-ready PDF and/or Markdown/text distribution after review.

## Quality boundaries

- Preserve behavior, meaningful content, brand identity, and existing framework except when explicitly approved.
- Distinguish inspected evidence from hypotheses and unverified issues.
- Avoid new dependencies and unrelated refactoring.
- Do not claim accessibility compliance or tests passed without verification.
- Each prompt must solve a different user problem.
- Never pad with extra prompts just for quantity.

## Release gate

Proofread all fifteen prompts, run controlled practical tests, fix verified defects, and export from one canonical source before describing the kit as tested or available for purchase.

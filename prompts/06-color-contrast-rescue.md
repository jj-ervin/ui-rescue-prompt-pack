# 06 — Color & Contrast Rescue

**Use when:** The palette is incoherent, emphasis is weak, or text appears difficult to read.

## Copy-paste prompt

Act as a digital color systems designer with accessibility expertise.

**Website or materials:** [INSERT]
**Established brand colors:** [INSERT, if any]
**Desired personality:** [OPTIONAL]

Inspect backgrounds/surfaces, text colors, calls to action, links, borders, status indicators, decorative accents, light/dark schemes and interactive states. Distinguish brand choices from usability defects.

First identify each observed issue and its location. Propose a compact color-role system: primary, secondary, accent, background, surface, primary/secondary text, borders, and status colors only where needed. Use HEX values when the underlying colors are known; label alternatives as proposals.

Assess relevant WCAG 2.2 AA **contrast** criteria when color samples can be reliably determined; calculate ratios and identify which pairings still need measurement. Note that contrast alone does not establish accessibility conformance.

Return a before/after role table, expected design benefit, risk to brand identity, and verification steps. Preserve established brand colors where practical. **Stop for approval before edits.**

## Working rules
- Inspect actual available materials; distinguish proven issues from possible ones.
- Preserve functionality, brand, content meaning, framework, and working integrations absent explicit approval.
- Do not invent measurements, verification results, or conformance claims.

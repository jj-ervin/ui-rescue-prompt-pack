# 02 — Generic AI Design Detector

**Use when:** A site looks templated or indistinguishable from many other AI-built pages.

## Copy-paste prompt

Act as a visual designer who specializes in detecting generic, inconsistent, or forgettable web design.

**Website or screenshots:** [INSERT]
**Brand personality:** [INSERT or 'not defined']

Inspect actual examples of repetitive card grids, decorative gradients/glows/shadows, predictable hero composition, unnecessary badges/icons, repeated sections, generic headings/CTAs, inconsistent imagery, and poor content differentiation.

Do **not** treat gradients, cards, rounded corners, or minimalism as inherently bad. Judge suitability for this site's audience and intent.

For each genuine issue, show the exact location, visual evidence, why the pattern hurts this site, a practical alternative, and whether change is essential or optional. Then propose one cohesive visual direction that preserves the site's recognizable identity. Prefer purposeful composition, typography and information design to adding decoration.

Include three short 'keep as-is' observations, if warranted, so the review isn't biased toward changing everything.

Do not create a new brand or edit the site. **Stop for approval.**

## Working rules
- Examine only materials actually accessible; separate observed defects from suspected risks.
- Preserve existing functionality, routes, content meaning, brand identity and architecture unless explicitly authorized.
- Do not invent inspection, test results, evidence, testimonials, or accessibility compliance.
- If changes need code access, do not claim they were made without editing and verifying them.

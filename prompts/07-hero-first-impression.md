# 07 — Hero Section & First Impression

**Use when:** The top of the homepage feels generic or unclear.

## Copy-paste prompt

Act as a landing-page designer focused on visual communication and clarity.

**Website or screenshots:** [INSERT]
**Target visitor:** [INSERT]
**Primary visitor action:** [INSERT]

Inspect headline, subtitle, CTA hierarchy, imagery, layout, typography, spacing, background and mobile composition. Evaluate whether a visitor can infer what the site is, who it serves, and what to do next. Treat this as a heuristic, not observed user research.

Propose **three distinct but brand-consistent directions**: (A) minimal/clarity-first, (B) brand-forward, (C) action-oriented. For each provide headline direction, supporting message, CTA behavior, layout description, mobile treatment, and tradeoffs. Do not invent testimonials, statistics, guarantees or capabilities.

Recommend one option based on actual content and goals. Identify anything needed before implementing. Preserve existing navigation and functionality. **Do not implement until I select a direction.**

## Working rules
- Inspect actual available materials; distinguish proven issues from possible ones.
- Preserve functionality, brand, content meaning, framework, and working integrations absent explicit approval.
- Do not invent measurements, verification results, or conformance claims.

# Bonus 01 — Quick Rescue: One-Shot Makeover

**Use when:** You want one concise, high-value design improvement workflow.

## Copy-paste prompt

Act as a senior UI/UX designer and cautious frontend assistant.

**Website/materials:** [INSERT]
**Audience and goal:** [INSERT]

Inspect only what you can actually access, then identify the **five most valuable visual issues**. Consider first impression, hierarchy, typography, spacing, color, navigation, mobile usability and accessibility. For each, cite specific observed evidence, recommend a concrete change, explain its expected benefit and how to verify it. Identify what remains unknown.

Preserve existing functionality, branding, factual content and framework. Don't add dependencies, unrelated features, decorative complexity or speculative claims.

First present a concise ordered improvement plan. **Wait for my approval before editing.** If you have no editing capability, give implementation-ready steps. After any approved implementation, compare with the original and report real checks and unverified aspects. Stop when the approved work is done.
